Settings and data

Nothing is typed in or saved. Price, daily history, cycle tops and the halving estimate all load automatically. To change the past cycle dates, edit the PAST table at the top of the script in index.html.

How it works

The cycle top is the highest daily close since the last halving. It counts as confirmed once price has fallen 20% from it. Time score = days since the top divided by the median past top-to-bottom length. Depth score = current drawdown divided by the median past drawdown. Phases use days since the last halving. The next halving date comes from the live Bitcoin block height, falling back to a table estimate. When today passes the next halving, a new ring starts by itself.

Descriptive tool, not financial advice. Three past cycles is a very small sample. The time clock assumes this cycle rhymes with earlier ones, and that may not hold.
