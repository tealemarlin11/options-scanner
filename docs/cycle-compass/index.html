Settings and data

Price, daily history, cycle tops, the halving estimate and the realized price all load automatically. The only box you can type in is the realized price backup, and it is ignored whenever live data is available. To change the past cycle dates, edit the PAST table at the top of the script in index.html.

How it works

The cycle top is the highest daily close since the last halving. It counts as confirmed once price has fallen 20% from it. Time score = days since the top divided by the median past top-to-bottom length. Depth score = current drawdown divided by the median past drawdown. Phases use days since the last halving. The next halving date comes from the live Bitcoin block height, falling back to a table estimate. When today passes the next halving, a new ring starts by itself.

Realized price is the average price at which every coin last moved on-chain, so roughly the market's cost basis (realized cap divided by supply). Providers calculate it slightly differently, so figures can vary by a few percent. The caution zone is the floor up to 1.25 times the floor. Past bear lows have been reported at or below this level, but that is only a few cycles, and price can stay above it for a long time.

Descriptive tool, not financial advice. Three past cycles is a very small sample. The time clock assumes this cycle rhymes with earlier ones, and that may not hold.
