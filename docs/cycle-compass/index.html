Settings and inputs

Price comes live from Coinbase in your browser. Monthly closes are also built in your browser from Coinbase daily candles. If either call fails, the saved data/prices.json and then a built-in snapshot from 10 Oct 2026 are used. Past gaps and drawdowns are approximate values from memory.

How it works

Time score = days since the last top divided by the median past top-to-bottom length. Depth score = current drawdown divided by the median past drawdown. Phases use days since the last halving, with the top and projected bottom as anchors. The halving date is an estimate based on block pace and will drift by a few days.

Descriptive tool, not financial advice. Three past cycles is a very small sample. The time clock assumes this cycle rhymes with earlier ones, and that may not hold.
